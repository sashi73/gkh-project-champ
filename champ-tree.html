Project Champ as a circuit tree

The foundations are the roots, the core and its menu are the trunk, each area of the app is a branch, and its features are the leaves. Whether your team works Agile, Scrum, Kanban or Lean, guided by PMBOK, Champ powers it. Tap a branch, or its name in the key, to send current through what it powers and see everything in it.
