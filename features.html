The power of AI

AI does the project admin.
People lead the project.

Champ is an AI agent built into every project. It watches, drafts, answers and warns, so project managers spend their day deciding, not compiling. Here is one working day.

  1. 7:00
    The watch

    Champ reviews every project before anyone logs in.

    Schedule, cost, risks, issues and billing, checked against the baseline, every morning.

    ●Hospital wing CPI 0.87, fit-out labour over budget
    ●ERP rollout testing 4 days behind
    ●11 projects on track
  2. 7:05
    The brief

    The project manager opens a project to a brief, not a blank screen.

    What changed overnight, what is at risk, and the three things to do first.

    Today on ERP rollout
    1. Move two testers from Training to Testing
    2. Ask the vendor for the patch date
    3. Tell the sponsor go-live holds if test ends Friday
  3. 9:30
    From a document

    A 60-page tender becomes a plan to check.

    Tasks, milestones, risks, budget and stakeholders, drafted from the document or a BIM model.

    🗓 46 tasks◆ 6 milestones⚠ 8 risks💰 $1.24M👥 12 stakeholders
  4. 11:00
    Ask in any language

    The program director asks in Malay; the answer comes with the numbers.

    Ask AI turns a question into a query over your own data, then into a chart or a table.

    “Projek mana yang lewat lebih seminggu?”
    3 projects · chart · table · PDF
  5. 14:00
    Change, costed

    A meeting note becomes a change request with its impact.

    Time, cost and risk worked out for the change board, before anything moves.

    CR-014 second lift core · +6 days · +$124,000 · needs the board
  6. 16:30
    The report

    The weekly status report writes itself from the live numbers.

    Word or PDF, with the RAG, milestones, risks and decisions; the project manager edits and sends.

    Status report · week 47
    Amber · SPI 0.94 · 2 risks up · next milestone 3 Dec
  7. 17:00
    The paperwork

    Contracts filed, redacted copies shared.

    Amounts, bank details and contacts blacked out for the team; the finance copy stays in the vault.

    Contract sum $250,000 · Pay to BSB 062-000 · Scope: build the clinic
